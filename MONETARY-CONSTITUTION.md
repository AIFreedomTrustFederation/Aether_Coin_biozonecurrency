# Aetherion Monetary Constitution

## Article I — Equal economic standing

Every eligible person has the same constitutional baseline issuance right per epoch. Wealth, account age, founder status, validator status, institutional office, ancestry, nationality, creed, profession, or previous contribution may not multiply that baseline right.

## Article II — No terminal supply cap

AetherCoin has no protocol-level terminal supply cap. At every finite moment supply remains finite and auditable. New supply may enter only through enumerated authorized transitions.

## Article III — No premine or private genesis allocation

The production genesis must contain zero founder, investor, team, adviser, private-sale, and hidden treasury balances. Any launch artifact containing such an allocation fails constitutional validation.

## Article IV — Authorized issuance classes

Permitted issuance classes are:

1. **Universal issuance** — equal baseline issuance for an eligible unique person.
2. **Contribution issuance** — governed, budgeted issuance for evidenced contribution.
3. **Regenerative issuance** — governed, budgeted issuance for evidenced ecological or social restoration.
4. **Stewardship issuance** — governed, budgeted compensation for network or public infrastructure service.

No other mint class exists without constitutional amendment.

## Article V — Human worth is not a score

Contribution programs may evaluate whether evidence satisfies a published program rule. They may not define a universal score of human worth, spiritual worthiness, civic dignity, or right to baseline economic participation.

## Article VI — Demurrage

The protocol may retire a small deterministic fraction of transferable balances per epoch according to a public parameter. Demurrage may not be secretly redirected to founders, validators, administrators, or private treasuries. Changes to the rate require ordinary governance within constitutional bounds and public simulation.

## Article VII — No token-weighted constitutional power

ATC balance cannot determine human constitutional vote weight, validator voting power, identity eligibility, legal personhood, or access to due process.

## Article VIII — Consensus independence

Consensus power is an infrastructure authorization. It must not compound automatically from ATC ownership. The target validator model assigns equal unit voting power to independently authorized active validators, subject to the consensus engine's requirements.

## Article IX — Resource accounting independence

Finite compute, bandwidth, storage, and transaction capacity are protected through non-transferable resource accounting, rate limits, quotas, and congestion controls. The protocol-level Pulse resource credit is not money and cannot confer governance or validator power.

## Article X — Privacy and uniqueness

The network may require evidence of unique eligibility for universal issuance. It must minimize public disclosure and should support privacy-preserving uniqueness proofs. Raw civil identity is not required to be permanently published on-chain.

## Article XI — Due process

Suspension of issuance eligibility, validator authorization, program accreditation, or identity attestation must have a recorded reason, review path, and appeal process. Emergency action must expire unless ratified.

## Article XII — No administrative balance seizure by default

Ordinary administrators may not rewrite balances. Any exceptional recovery or legal-compliance mechanism must be explicit, narrowly scoped, publicly specified, and incapable of silently becoming a universal seizure key.

## Article XIII — Transparent budgets

Contribution, regenerative, and stewardship issuance require epoch-bounded budgets. Unused budgets do not automatically create supply. Evidence approval cannot mint above the remaining authorized budget.

## Article XIV — Supply accounting

For all valid states:

`circulating_supply = cumulative_authorized_issuance - cumulative_retirement`.

Ordinary transfers conserve supply.

## Article XV — No speculative promise

The protocol makes no guarantee that ATC appreciates, preserves purchasing power, tracks a fiat currency, or produces investment returns. Economic stability is an empirical problem requiring simulation, observation, and governance.

## Article XVI — Spiritual freedom and technical truth

The project may be spiritually inspired, but religious profession is not an economic eligibility requirement. Symbolic or doctrinal concepts are not cryptographic, biological, or physical measurements unless separately implemented and validated as such.

## Article XVII — Amendment protection

Changes to Articles I–III, VII–VIII, XII, XIV, and XVI are constitutional changes. They require multi-chamber supermajority approval, a public deliberation period, machine-readable diff, implementation review, and a timelock before activation.

## Article XVIII — Genesis equality across time

A person joining decades after launch receives the same constitutional baseline issuance rate then in force as another equally eligible person in that epoch. Early arrival creates history, not a higher class of person.

## Article XIX — Local sovereignty

Local trusts and communities may operate additional voluntary economic programs, provided those programs do not falsify canonical ATC issuance, secretly create validator power, or revoke the universal constitutional rights of participants in the wider network.

## Article XX — Covenant of administration

Protocol operators, developers, governance bodies, validators, attesters, and AI agents are bound by the same public rules they administer. Privileged technical access does not create privileged moral or economic standing.
