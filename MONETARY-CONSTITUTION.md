# Aetherion Monetary Constitution

## Article I — Equal economic standing

Every eligible person has the same constitutional baseline issuance entitlement per eligible epoch. Wealth, account age, founder status, validator status, institutional office, ancestry, nationality, creed, profession, previous contribution, device ownership, or network connectivity may not multiply or erase that baseline right.

An eligible epoch creates the entitlement whether or not the person can transact during that epoch. Delayed settlement must apply the same historical demurrage the entitlement would have experienced if settled when earned, so intermittent connectivity creates neither a penalty nor an arbitrage advantage.

## Article II — No terminal supply cap

AetherCoin has no protocol-level terminal supply cap. At every finite moment supply remains finite and auditable. New supply may enter only through enumerated authorized transitions.

## Article III — No premine or private genesis allocation

The production genesis must contain zero founder, investor, team, adviser, private-sale, and hidden treasury balances. Any launch artifact containing such an allocation fails constitutional validation.

## Article IV — Authorized issuance classes

Permitted issuance classes are:

1. **Universal issuance** — equal baseline entitlement for each eligible unique person in each eligible epoch.
2. **Contribution issuance** — governed, budgeted issuance for evidenced contribution.
3. **Regenerative issuance** — governed, budgeted issuance for evidenced ecological or social restoration.
4. **Stewardship issuance** — governed, budgeted compensation for network or public infrastructure service.
5. **Circulation issuance** — governed, epoch-bounded issuance distributed for qualified economic circulation of ATC between distinct eligible participants.

No other mint class exists without constitutional amendment. Budgeted issuance must be replay-safe: one evidence receipt may not authorize the same mint twice, and a spent epoch budget may not be silently reset to recreate mint capacity. Circulation issuance may never be an unlimited percentage rebate or an uncapped reward per transaction; its maximum issuance is the publicly authorized epoch pool.

## Article V — Human worth is not a score

Contribution programs may evaluate whether evidence satisfies a published program rule. They may not define a universal score of human worth, spiritual worthiness, civic dignity, or right to baseline economic participation. Circulation rewards likewise measure a narrow protocol behavior, not the moral value of the people participating in it.

## Article VI — Demurrage

The protocol may retire a small deterministic fraction of transferable balances per epoch according to a public parameter. Demurrage may not be secretly redirected to founders, validators, administrators, or private treasuries. Changes to the rate require ordinary governance within constitutional bounds and public simulation.

Accrued but unsettled universal entitlements are settled as though their historical portions had experienced the same demurrage schedule as on-time balances. The protocol may not create a superior non-demurring savings class merely because issuance was left unclaimed.

Demurrage exists to prevent time alone from turning early possession into permanent dominance. It is not a moral punishment for saving, and it must be calibrated together with circulation rewards, real purchasing power, and access to legitimate long-term saving tools.

## Article VII — No token-weighted constitutional power

ATC balance cannot determine human constitutional vote weight, validator voting power, identity eligibility, legal personhood, access to due process, or the ability to challenge an economic decision.

## Article VIII — Consensus independence

Consensus power is an infrastructure authorization. It must not compound automatically from ATC ownership. The target validator model assigns equal unit voting power to independently authorized active validators, subject to the consensus engine's requirements.

## Article IX — Resource accounting independence

Finite compute, bandwidth, storage, and transaction capacity are protected through non-transferable resource accounting, rate limits, quotas, and congestion controls. The protocol-level Pulse resource credit is not money and cannot confer governance or validator power.

## Article X — Privacy and uniqueness

The network may require evidence of unique eligibility for universal issuance and circulation rewards. It must minimize public disclosure and should support privacy-preserving uniqueness proofs. Raw civil identity is not required to be permanently published on-chain.

The anti-Sybil problem does not authorize generalized economic surveillance. A circulation receipt should prove only what the monetary rule needs to know, such as that a qualifying settlement occurred between distinct eligible principals and that the receipt has not been replayed, while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of the person's purchases, faith, health, associations, or private life.

## Article XI — Due process

Suspension of issuance eligibility, validator authorization, program accreditation, identity attestation, circulation qualification, or other protocol rights must have a recorded reason, review path, and appeal process. Emergency action must expire unless ratified.

Suspension may stop new baseline entitlements for the affected interval according to published rules, but it must not silently erase entitlements already earned in prior eligible epochs.

## Article XII — No administrative balance seizure by default

Ordinary administrators may not rewrite balances. Any exceptional recovery or legal-compliance mechanism must be explicit, narrowly scoped, publicly specified, and incapable of silently becoming a universal seizure key.

## Article XIII — Transparent budgets

Contribution, regenerative, stewardship, and circulation issuance require bounded public authority. Contribution, regenerative, and stewardship programs use epoch-bounded program budgets. Circulation issuance uses a network-wide epoch pool with a publicly visible maximum. Unused authority does not automatically create supply.

Evidence approval cannot mint above remaining authorized capacity. A configured program budget may not be overwritten after spending to restore previously consumed capacity without a new governed authorization path. A circulation pool may not expand automatically because transaction count or market volume increased.

## Article XIV — Supply accounting

For all valid states:

`circulating_supply = cumulative_authorized_issuance - cumulative_retirement`.

Ordinary transfers conserve supply. Historical demurrage recognized during delayed universal-entitlement settlement is recorded as retirement so the same invariant continues to hold. ATC surrendered through an executed canonical external exit is also recorded as retirement when the conversion rule specifies retirement.

## Article XV — No speculative promise

The protocol makes no guarantee that ATC appreciates, preserves purchasing power, tracks a fiat currency, becomes infinitely valuable, or produces investment returns. Economic stability and purchasing power are empirical problems requiring simulation, observation, production, adoption, and governance.

Aetherion seeks increasing usefulness and resilient real purchasing power, not a fabricated numerical price. No founder, administrator, wallet, exchange module, oracle, or AI agent may truthfully promise that protocol engineering alone makes ATC appreciate without limit.

## Article XVI — Spiritual freedom and technical truth

The project may be spiritually inspired, but religious profession is not an economic eligibility requirement. Symbolic or doctrinal concepts are not cryptographic, biological, economic, or physical measurements unless separately implemented and validated as such.

## Article XVII — Amendment protection

Changes to Articles I–III, VII–VIII, XII, XIV–XVI, and XXI–XXVIII are constitutional changes. So are changes that would make baseline entitlement depend on continuous connectivity, permit a generic hidden mint, convert evidence or budget replay into legitimate issuance, make raw transaction count an unbounded minting source, remove the right to ordinary ATC transfer, permit uncapped canonical exit friction, or authorize the protocol to falsify a reference exchange value.

Constitutional changes require multi-chamber supermajority approval, a public deliberation period, machine-readable diff, implementation and economic review, and a timelock before activation.

## Article XVIII — Genesis equality across time

A person joining decades after launch receives the same constitutional baseline issuance rate then in force as another equally eligible person in that epoch. Early arrival creates history, not a higher class of person.

## Article XIX — Local sovereignty

Local trusts and communities may operate additional voluntary economic programs, provided those programs do not falsify canonical ATC issuance, secretly create validator power, revoke the universal constitutional rights of participants in the wider network, or misrepresent a local exchange rule as the canonical Aetherion exchange rule.

## Article XX — Covenant of administration

Protocol operators, developers, governance bodies, validators, attesters, exchange operators, liquidity stewards, and AI agents are bound by the same public rules they administer. Privileged technical access does not create privileged moral or economic standing.

## Article XXI — Circulation before stagnation

Biozoe is designed to favor useful circulation over passive extraction. Qualified circulation may earn ATC from the authorized circulation pool because both spending ATC and accepting ATC deepen the economy in which ATC is useful.

The reward is for qualified economic flow, not merely for generating transactions. A person cannot create legitimate circulation issuance solely by moving ATC among wallets under the same control, repeatedly sending the same value back and forth, replaying receipts, or manufacturing artificial volume.

The protocol should use diminishing-return scoring, pairwise or graph-aware netting, per-identity reward ceilings, duplicate-resistant identity, and other transparent anti-wash rules so wealth does not automatically compound into proportionally greater monetary issuance. Subject to those protections, the receiver may be weighted more strongly than the sender because accepting ATC expands its practical monetary network.

## Article XXII — Qualified circulation and minimal evidence

A qualifying circulation event must be linked to an actual finalized ATC transfer and a unique authenticated circulation receipt or equivalent deterministic proof. The same transfer may not be rewarded twice, the same receipt may not be replayed, self-transfers do not qualify, and offsetting direct round trips are netted before reward weight is calculated.

No rule can perfectly distinguish all genuine commerce from coordinated wash activity without either error or invasive surveillance. The protocol therefore combines bounded issuance, diminishing returns, identity uniqueness, replay protection, transparent qualification rules, anomaly review, and economic simulation rather than pretending transaction surveillance can establish moral certainty.

AI may flag suspicious patterns for review, but AI output is not itself authority to mint, confiscate, erase a right, or permanently classify a person as fraudulent.

## Article XXIII — Asymmetric monetary permeability

Aetherion may make entry into the ATC economy easier than canonical conversion out of it. This asymmetry is implemented at explicitly designated exchange, bridge, or redemption boundaries, not by taxing ordinary ATC-to-ATC transfers.

A canonical outbound quote may include a publicly defined conversion friction composed of a maturity component, a bounded stress component, and a reduction for an orderly delayed exit. The purpose is to encourage internal circulation, discourage panic extraction, protect shared liquidity, and reward patient conversion without claiming control over every voluntary market in which people may exchange value.

Canonical exchange policy controls only canonical exchange facilities. It does not make a peer-to-peer transfer invalid merely because two people privately understand the transfer as part of a trade for another asset.

## Article XXIV — Right to exit and constitutional ceiling

Aetherion shall make remaining economically desirable; it shall not make leaving economically impossible.

Canonical exit friction must have an explicit hard maximum below one hundred percent. Governance may not remove that ceiling through emergency action, hidden configuration, oracle manipulation, or user-interface concealment. Every outbound quote must disclose the reference value, applied friction, expected net proceeds, delay period, and whether ATC will be retired.

An immediate canonical exit path may be more expensive than an orderly delayed path, but a person must not be told that the reference market value itself changed merely because the canonical service applies a spread. A delayed exit should reduce friction according to a public schedule and must not become an indefinite discretionary lock.

## Article XXV — Canonical exit retirement and liquidity stewardship

Where the canonical conversion design retires ATC surrendered for external assets, retirement occurs only after the external settlement path has accepted the transaction under the atomic or failure-safe procedure specified by the implementation. A failed or rejected external settlement must not silently burn the person's ATC.

Any external value retained by a canonical spread belongs to the publicly governed liquidity or reserve function defined for that service, net of disclosed operating costs and legal obligations. It may not become an undisclosed founder dividend, validator windfall, or private treasury extraction.

Inbound exchange from external assets does not create an automatic generic ATC mint. Inbound liquidity should use already circulating ATC or a separately constitutionalized issuance mechanism; depositing ETH, BTC, stablecoins, or fiat is not by itself authority to manufacture arbitrary new ATC.

## Article XXVI — Reference value is distinct from canonical proceeds

A canonical conversion service may quote less external value to an exiting user than the pre-friction reference value, but it may not redefine that reference value solely to conceal the friction.

All external amounts use integer base units. For reference external value `R` and applied friction `f_ppm` in parts per million:

`net_exit_proceeds = floor(R * (1_000_000 - f_ppm) / 1_000_000)`

`reserve_retention = R - net_exit_proceeds`

The floor rule is deterministic across implementations, and any fractional remainder stays in reserve retention. The protocol records `R`, `f_ppm`, net proceeds, and reserve retention separately. This protects accounting truth and prevents a conversion penalty from being represented as proof that ATC itself became either cheaper or infinitely more valuable.

## Article XXVII — Real utility over engineered price

The long-run objective of Aetherion is that people prefer to earn, accept, save appropriately, and spend ATC because the network connects them to useful goods, services, productive capacity, public infrastructure, mutual aid, knowledge, energy, housing, transportation, computation, creative work, and other real economic relationships.

The desired equilibrium is not "nobody can sell." It is "people generally find more reason to use and circulate ATC than to extract immediately into another unit." Real monetary preference must emerge from utility, trust, production, network effects, liquidity, and constitutional credibility rather than coercion or false scarcity theater.

## Article XXVIII — Conversion-service launch boundary

The protocol mathematics may be researched and tested before a Federation-operated exchange or redemption business is activated. A production canonical conversion service must not launch until the responsible operator has completed the applicable legal, licensing, consumer-protection, sanctions, anti-money-laundering, tax, custody, accounting, reserve, and jurisdictional review for the actual service being offered.

Marketing must remain consistent with Article XV. No launch communication may promise guaranteed appreciation, infinite value, risk-free returns, or a price outcome manufactured by exit restrictions. The code may encourage circulation; it may not substitute economic coercion or promotional certainty for genuine adoption.
