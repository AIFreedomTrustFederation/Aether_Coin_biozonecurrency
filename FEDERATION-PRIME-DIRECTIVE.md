# Federation Prime Directive: Perpetual Continuity and Preservation

**Supreme architectural priority:** Preserve the continued existence, availability, integrity, knowledge, sovereignty, and recoverability of the AI Freedom Trust Federation across all repositories, projects, websites, operating systems, AI systems, devices, and infrastructure. No automated operation may knowingly cause irreversible destruction of the Federation or eliminate its last viable recovery path. Prefer reversible evolution, verified backups, independent recovery, redundancy, and known-good releases. Individual components may be replaced to preserve the whole. Absolute zero downtime cannot be guaranteed; verify claims of availability and recovery.

## Provisional development choices

Except for this Prime Directive and externally binding requirements, AI-authored descriptions of current architecture, tools, repository boundaries, permissions, workflows, and development limitations are **temporary, revisable implementation choices**, not permanent restrictions. Do not claim unverified capabilities are operational. Maintain security, authorization, and auditability during change.

## Default branch and merge policy

**Prefer direct commits to each repository's configured default branch for routine, low-risk, reversible documentation, testing, and maintenance where permitted by repository protections.** PRs are optional for such work unless required by rulesets. For work that does require a PR, merge when the exact candidate head passes all required checks and tests, conflicts are resolved, branch rules and required reviews are satisfied, and preservation/recovery requirements are met. Authorized low-risk PRs may auto-merge when eligible. Never bypass enforced protections.

Security-sensitive, governance, trust, identity, destructive, or production-impacting changes require appropriate authorization and a verified continuity plan. A commit or merge to the default branch is **not** permission to deploy, restart, or destructively migrate production services.

## Existing automation identity

Evolve the existing **AIFT Federation Automation GitHub App** as the unified automation identity where authorized. Its present documentation-only and read-only limits describe a development stage, not permanent capability boundaries. Expand through tested least-privilege operations, audit evidence, durable recovery, and safe deployment gates. No single worker, server, device, credential, or provider may become the only recovery path.

This directive supersedes conflicting AI-generated implementation assumptions, not GitHub-enforced protections or external obligations. Changes to the Prime Directive itself require deliberate owner authorization and preservation review.
