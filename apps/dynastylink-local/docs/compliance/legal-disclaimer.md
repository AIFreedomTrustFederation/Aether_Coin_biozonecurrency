The app may help users organize information, draft mission language, prepare covenant statements, map assets, identify beneficiaries, assign stewardship roles, upload supporting files, and generate a Federated Trust Identity Packet. Users should consult qualified attorneys, tax professionals, financial advisors, insurance professionals, and other licensed professionals before creating, funding, modifying, or relying on any trust structure.
