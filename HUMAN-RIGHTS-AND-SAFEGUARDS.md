# Aetherion Human Rights and Safeguards

Aetherion is an economic coordination system. Because economic systems can become systems of coercion, the protocol must define what it refuses to optimize away. A currency that rewards circulation or discourages extraction must still preserve the dignity, agency, privacy, and informed consent of the person using it.

## 1. Equal dignity

No account balance, contribution score, circulation score, validator role, governance office, AI assessment, social reputation, religious profession, citizenship, ancestry, disability, health status, employment status, or market productivity determines a person's basic human worth.

Universal issuance eligibility may require unique-person verification. It may not require proving economic usefulness.

## 2. No universal social-credit score

Aetherion must not collapse behavior, relationships, faith, politics, purchases, employment, disputes, health, community reputation, or circulation activity into one universal score governing access to ordinary economic life.

Program-specific evidence may answer a narrow question such as whether a published contribution or circulation condition was satisfied. That evidence must not silently become a permanent global rank.

## 3. No forced public identity

The chain should require the minimum information necessary to establish a right.

Unique-person issuance and circulation qualification should move toward privacy-preserving credentials, selective disclosure, and plural attestation. Raw government identifiers, biometric templates, home addresses, medical records, family relationships, religious information, or complete purchase histories should not be permanently exposed on a public ledger merely to receive ordinary economic standing or circulation rewards.

## 4. No single identity oracle

No government, corporation, church, biometric vendor, AI provider, Federation administrator, exchange operator, or validator cartel should possess permanent unilateral authority to decide who counts as a person.

Identity must support multiple attestation paths, recovery, challenge, appeal, and transparent revocation rules.

## 5. No forced biometrics

Biometrics may be one optional evidence mechanism in a future identity ecosystem only after privacy, consent, bias, coercion, breach, revocation, and accessibility risks are addressed.

Aetherion should preserve non-biometric paths to ordinary participation wherever technically and legally possible.

## 6. Due process and restoration

Eligibility suspension, program disqualification, validator removal, governance sanctions, circulation qualification disputes, or exchange-service restrictions require a published rule, a stated reason, notice where safety permits, a review path, an appeal path, proportionality, and expiration or reconsideration where appropriate.

Permanent punishment should not be the default response to recoverable failure. Suspension of future eligibility must not silently confiscate baseline entitlements already earned during earlier eligible epochs.

## 7. No balance-derived citizenship

Owning more ATC does not create more human civic standing. Buying tokens does not buy constitutional personhood, votes, validator power, identity priority, circulation priority, or superior access to appeals.

## 8. Accessibility

Wallets, governance, identity recovery, circulation explanations, and exchange quotes should be designed for people with different cognitive, sensory, motor, literacy, language, and technical abilities.

Critical actions require plain-language explanations in addition to technical detail. Accessibility is a protocol adoption requirement, not decorative UI polish.

## 9. Children and dependent persons

The system must not treat minors or legally dependent people as exploitable issuance or circulation identities.

Future production policy must define guardianship, transition to independent control, privacy, inheritance, abuse prevention, reward qualification, and recovery without creating permanent guardian ownership of another person's economic identity.

## 10. AI cannot own consent

AI may assist with explanation, translation, anomaly detection, planning, simulation, fraud review, and proposal drafting.

AI cannot infer permission to sign a transfer, expose a seed phrase, surrender custody, waive an appeal, change identity status, vote a constitutional right, accept a financial contract, authorize a canonical conversion, publish private evidence, or permanently classify a person as a wash trader.

High-impact actions require an authenticated principal operating under explicit authority.

## 11. Right to understandable transactions

Before signing, a person should be able to know what action is being requested, which network it affects, what amount or right changes, who receives value or authority, whether the action is reversible, what evidence or permission is being disclosed, and what fees or resource costs apply.

Dark patterns, hidden approvals, bundled unrelated consent, and intentionally confusing signing flows violate this principle.

## 12. Right to local exit

Federation should mean interoperability without erasure.

Where technically possible, local trusts and communities should be able to leave a service provider, validator relationship, hosting provider, AI provider, or local federation arrangement without surrendering canonical identity history or lawful economic ownership.

## 13. No artificial paywall to basic protocol standing

ATC and Pulse are separated so a person does not need accumulated wealth merely to have basic access to ordinary protocol participation.

Congestion and finite infrastructure still require resource controls. Those controls should include fair-use allowances and transparent limits rather than converting every fundamental action into an auction among the richest users.

## 14. Right to intermittent connectivity

Economic standing must not depend on perfect internet access, continuous smartphone ownership, stable housing, uninterrupted electricity, or the ability to submit a transaction every epoch.

For every epoch in which a person is validly eligible, the baseline entitlement accrues independently of whether that person can immediately settle it. When a delayed entitlement is eventually settled, its historical portions receive the same deterministic demurrage they would have experienced if settled on time. This preserves the right without creating a privileged non-demurring account for people who wait.

Temporary validator censorship or service-provider outage should therefore delay settlement rather than permanently destroy an otherwise earned baseline entitlement.

## 15. Freedom of conscience

Aetherion's founders may openly locate the project's moral purpose in service to the Most High and the Kingdom virtues of truth, mercy, justice, humility, stewardship, restoration, and love of neighbor.

Participation in the general economic protocol does not require assent to that theology. The system must not assign economic rank based on creed, worship, conversion, doctrine, or spiritual conformity.

## 16. Epistemic honesty

Users have a right to know whether a claim is implemented, prototype, planned, independently audited, empirical, interpretive, philosophical, doctrinal, or aspirational.

Symbolic language cannot be used to manufacture technical authority. Exchange friction cannot be described as proof that ATC gained value, and a circulation reward cannot be described as evidence that the rewarded person is morally superior.

## 17. No secret monetary administration

All canonical issuance classes, retirement rules, circulation pools, conversion-friction parameters, budgets, and parameter changes must be publicly inspectable at the protocol level. There is no legitimate hidden founder mint, silent balance rewrite, undisclosed inflation path, replayed evidence mint, replayed circulation receipt, silent restoration of a spent program budget, or hidden exit spread.

## 18. Security without surveillance maximalism

Fraud prevention does not automatically justify collecting every possible datum.

Security design should prefer cryptographic proof, scoped authorization, rate limits, threshold controls, local processing, selective disclosure, pairwise netting, bounded reward pools, and audit trails over generalized behavioral surveillance.

The anti-wash problem is real, but the solution cannot be a system that permanently records every person's private purchases and associations merely to determine whether they deserve a small circulation reward.

## 19. Right to challenge automation

If automated systems materially influence identity, program eligibility, circulation qualification, fraud flags, conversion access, or governance workflows, affected people must have a path to human review appropriate to the risk.

An AI confidence score is evidence for review, not a final moral verdict.

## 20. Open protocol accountability

Consensus rules, monetary policy code, circulation policy, canonical conversion policy, genesis inputs, governance procedures, and security assumptions should remain publicly inspectable. Operational secrets such as private keys stay secret; rules that exercise public power do not.

## 21. Right to ordinary ATC transfer

Aetherion does not impose canonical exit friction on an ordinary native ATC-to-ATC transfer merely because one person sends value to another. Commerce, gifts, payroll, local settlement, family transfers, cooperative exchange, and peer-to-peer ownership movement remain distinct from a Federation-operated redemption into an external asset.

The protocol should not attempt to preserve exchange value by turning every wallet recipient into a classified destination or by requiring permission to send ATC to another lawful holder.

## 22. Right to bounded and disclosed canonical exit

A person using a canonical conversion service has the right to know the reference external value, every component of the applied friction, the constitutional maximum, the expected proceeds, the chosen delay, the retirement behavior of the surrendered ATC, and any additional operator fees before authorizing the conversion.

Canonical exit friction must remain below the constitutional hard ceiling. The service may encourage a patient exit with a lower spread, but it may not create an indefinite discretionary lock or pretend that the reference value itself fell solely because the user chose to leave.

## 23. Freedom from trapped-liquidity economics

Aetherion may make remaining inside the ATC economy attractive. It may not define success as making exit economically impossible.

The protocol cannot guarantee control over private markets, and it should not try to obtain such control by criminalizing ordinary transfers or building generalized financial surveillance. Its stronger path is to make ATC useful enough that internal circulation is usually preferable to immediate extraction.

## 24. Right to circulate without becoming a product

A person may earn a bounded circulation reward for qualified use of ATC without surrendering ownership of their complete economic life to the network.

A production circulation receipt should prove the minimum necessary facts and should not require the protocol to know the theology, politics, health condition, intimate relationship, or full itemized purchase history behind an otherwise lawful transaction.

## 25. The test

Aetherion should repeatedly ask a simple question:

**Does this feature help people coordinate value while preserving their dignity and agency, or does it make the system more powerful by making the person more dependent?**

When those aims conflict, the burden of proof belongs on the expansion of protocol power. A monetary design that can only retain users by removing meaningful choice has failed the human purpose of Biozoe even if the code still runs.
