# Aetherion Human Rights and Safeguards

Aetherion is an economic coordination system. Because economic systems can become systems of coercion, the protocol must define what it refuses to optimize away.

## 1. Equal dignity

No account balance, contribution score, validator role, governance office, AI assessment, social reputation, religious profession, citizenship, ancestry, disability, health status, employment status, or market productivity determines a person's basic human worth.

Universal issuance eligibility may require unique-person verification. It may not require proving economic usefulness.

## 2. No universal social-credit score

Aetherion must not collapse behavior, relationships, faith, politics, purchases, employment, disputes, health, or community reputation into one universal score governing access to ordinary economic life.

Program-specific evidence may answer a narrow question such as whether a published contribution condition was satisfied. That evidence must not silently become a permanent global rank.

## 3. No forced public identity

The chain should require the minimum information necessary to establish a right.

Unique-person issuance should move toward privacy-preserving credentials, selective disclosure, and plural attestation. Raw government identifiers, biometric templates, home addresses, medical records, family relationships, or religious information should not be permanently exposed on a public ledger merely to receive ordinary economic standing.

## 4. No single identity oracle

No government, corporation, church, biometric vendor, AI provider, Federation administrator, or validator cartel should possess permanent unilateral authority to decide who counts as a person.

Identity must support multiple attestation paths, recovery, challenge, appeal, and transparent revocation rules.

## 5. No forced biometrics

Biometrics may be one optional evidence mechanism in a future identity ecosystem only after privacy, consent, bias, coercion, breach, revocation, and accessibility risks are addressed.

Aetherion should preserve non-biometric paths to ordinary participation wherever technically and legally possible.

## 6. Due process and restoration

Eligibility suspension, program disqualification, validator removal, or governance sanctions require:

- a recorded rule,
- a stated reason,
- notice where safety permits,
- a review path,
- an appeal path,
- proportionality,
- expiration or reconsideration where appropriate.

Permanent punishment should not be the default response to recoverable failure.

## 7. No balance-derived citizenship

Owning more ATC does not create more human civic standing. Buying tokens does not buy constitutional personhood, votes, validator power, identity priority, or superior access to appeals.

## 8. Accessibility

Wallets, governance, identity recovery, and economic explanations should be designed for people with different cognitive, sensory, motor, literacy, language, and technical abilities.

Critical actions require plain-language explanations in addition to technical detail. Accessibility is a protocol adoption requirement, not decorative UI polish.

## 9. Children and dependent persons

The system must not treat minors or legally dependent people as exploitable issuance identities.

Future production policy must define guardianship, transition to independent control, privacy, inheritance, abuse prevention, and recovery without creating permanent guardian ownership of another person's economic identity.

## 10. AI cannot own consent

AI may assist with explanation, translation, anomaly detection, planning, simulation, and proposal drafting.

AI cannot infer permission to:

- sign a transfer,
- expose a seed phrase,
- surrender custody,
- waive an appeal,
- change identity status,
- vote a constitutional right,
- accept a financial contract,
- or publish private evidence.

High-impact actions require an authenticated principal operating under explicit authority.

## 11. Right to understandable transactions

Before signing, a person should be able to know:

- what action is being requested,
- which network it affects,
- what amount or right changes,
- who receives value or authority,
- whether the action is reversible,
- what evidence or permission is being disclosed,
- what fees or resource costs apply.

Dark patterns, hidden approvals, bundled unrelated consent, and intentionally confusing signing flows violate this principle.

## 12. Right to local exit

Federation should mean interoperability without erasure.

Where technically possible, local trusts and communities should be able to leave a service provider, validator relationship, hosting provider, AI provider, or local federation arrangement without surrendering canonical identity history or lawful economic ownership.

## 13. No artificial paywall to basic protocol standing

ATC and Pulse are separated so a person does not need accumulated wealth merely to have basic access to ordinary protocol participation.

Congestion and finite infrastructure still require resource controls. Those controls should include fair-use allowances and transparent limits rather than converting every fundamental action into an auction among the richest users.

## 14. Freedom of conscience

Aetherion's founders may openly locate the project's moral purpose in service to the Most High and the Kingdom virtues of truth, mercy, justice, humility, stewardship, restoration, and love of neighbor.

Participation in the general economic protocol does not require assent to that theology. The system must not assign economic rank based on creed, worship, conversion, doctrine, or spiritual conformity.

## 15. Epistemic honesty

Users have a right to know whether a claim is:

- implemented,
- prototype,
- planned,
- independently audited,
- empirical,
- interpretive,
- philosophical,
- doctrinal,
- aspirational.

Symbolic language cannot be used to manufacture technical authority.

## 16. No secret monetary administration

All canonical issuance classes, retirement rules, budgets, and parameter changes must be publicly inspectable at the protocol level. There is no legitimate hidden founder mint, silent balance rewrite, or undisclosed inflation path.

## 17. Security without surveillance maximalism

Fraud prevention does not automatically justify collecting every possible datum.

Security design should prefer cryptographic proof, scoped authorization, rate limits, threshold controls, local processing, selective disclosure, and audit trails over generalized behavioral surveillance.

## 18. Right to challenge automation

If automated systems materially influence identity, program eligibility, fraud flags, or governance workflows, affected people must have a path to human review appropriate to the risk.

An AI confidence score is evidence for review, not a final moral verdict.

## 19. Open protocol accountability

Consensus rules, monetary policy code, genesis inputs, governance procedures, and security assumptions should remain publicly inspectable. Operational secrets such as private keys stay secret; rules that exercise public power do not.

## 20. The test

Aetherion should repeatedly ask a simple question:

**Does this feature help people coordinate value while preserving their dignity and agency, or does it make the system more powerful by making the person more dependent?**

When those aims conflict, the burden of proof belongs on the expansion of protocol power.
